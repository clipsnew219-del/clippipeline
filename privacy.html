Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

ClipPipeline is a private content management and publishing tool used to prepare and publish short-form video content to authorized social media accounts.

TikTok Integration

ClipPipeline may access basic TikTok account information and use authorization granted by the account owner to upload and publish video content through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Data Use

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to provide the requested account authorization and content publishing functionality. ClipPipeline does not sell TikTok user data to third parties.

Data Retention

Authorization information is retained only as necessary to operate the integration and may be removed when access is revoked or the integration is no longer used.
