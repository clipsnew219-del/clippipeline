Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClipPipeline ("we", "the tool") processes when an account owner connects a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, and how that information is used, stored and deleted.

1. Who is responsible

ClipPipeline is developed and operated by an independent creator. Contact: clipsnew219@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account identifiers: the TikTok open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar) returned by TikTok after authorization.
  • Access tokens: the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube or Instagram after the account owner grants permission.
  • Publishing data: the video files, captions and post IDs or links of clips published through the tool, plus the publishing status returned by the platform.

We do not collect TikTok passwords, messages, contacts, followers' data or viewing history.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to authorize the connected account, upload and publish the creator's own clips, check the publishing status and keep a record of what was published. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

ClipPipeline runs on the account owner's own computer. Tokens and publishing records are stored locally on that computer and are only sent to the respective platform's official API over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok user data with third parties. Data is only exchanged with the platform the content is being published to.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and are deleted when access is revoked or the integration is removed. Publishing records are kept so that duplicates can be avoided and can be deleted on request.

You can revoke ClipPipeline's access to TikTok at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. To request deletion of any stored data, email clipsnew219@gmail.com.

7. Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site.

8. Children

ClipPipeline is not directed at children and must only be used with accounts that are permitted under the respective platform's terms.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
