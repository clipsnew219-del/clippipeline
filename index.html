From campaign footage to ready-to-post clips.

ClipPipeline is a desktop publishing tool for short-form video creators. It turns licensed campaign footage into vertical clips, checks every clip against the campaign's rules and publishes it to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

See how it works Privacy Policy

How it works

ClipPipeline runs on the creator's own computer. Every step happens locally, and the creator stays in control of what gets published.

Step 1

Import footage

The creator adds official footage provided by a brand or clipping campaign. Only material the campaign allows to be used is processed.

Step 2

Create clips

Speech is transcribed, the strongest moment is selected and the clip is cut to 9:16 with readable captions.

Step 3

Check the rules

Each clip is checked against the campaign requirements, such as length, required hashtags, disclosures and forbidden topics. Unclear clips go to manual review.

Step 4

Publish

Approved clips are published to the creator's own connected accounts with the required caption and disclosure.

Step 5

Track

The tool records where each clip was published, so the creator can submit the post to the campaign and avoid duplicates.

Step 6

Stay compliant

Clips are never posted twice to the same account, and nothing is published without passing the checks.

TikTok integration

ClipPipeline uses TikTok's official Content Posting API and Login Kit. The account owner signs in with TikTok and explicitly authorizes the tool before anything can be uploaded.

Sign in with TikTok

Authorization uses TikTok's OAuth flow. ClipPipeline never sees or stores the TikTok password.

Upload and publish

Approved clips are uploaded as video posts with their caption. The creator's privacy and interaction settings are respected.

Revoke any time

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

Supported platforms

Clips are published only to accounts the creator owns and has connected.
